Privacy notice

Last updated 2 November 2026. FrescoKart is a working name, so this notice is short and plain.

Who we are

We are the team behind FrescoKart, a delivery service for ready-to-cook fish kits in Kochi. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +91 81578 63503.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, when you place an order or ask us to message you when we reach your area.
  • Your name, house name or landmark and a note, only if you choose to give them.
  • Your area and what you ordered, including quantities and prices shown to you.
  • Which area you tapped on the page, even if you do not leave a number. We count this once per visitor per area per day.
  • How you found us, for example the ad or link you came from, and your browser type. We do not store your IP address. We keep a scrambled code that changes every day, only so the same visitor is not counted many times.

Why we use it

  • To confirm your order, the price and your delivery time.
  • To message you when we start delivering in your area, if you asked us to.
  • To see which areas want us most, so we open where it makes sense.
  • To fix problems with the page and with your order.

We contact you only about these things, by WhatsApp or phone. We do not sell your details and we do not give them to advertisers.

Who can see it

The FrescoKart team can see it, and so can the company that hosts our server. If we use a rider or a delivery partner for your order, they see your name, address and phone number for that delivery only.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them for the reasons above. If we stop delivering in your area, we delete the sign-ups for that area.

Your choices

  • Ask us to show you, correct or delete your details by messaging us on WhatsApp.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. Reply STOP to any message, or tell us on WhatsApp.

Cookies and tracking

This page does not use advertising cookies. It may keep a small note in your browser for the current visit, so your area vote is not sent twice.
